# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that keep this a good place to work:

- being welcoming to people whose experience differs from yours
- taking criticism of your code as being about the code
- saying what you mean directly, and assuming others are doing the same
- accepting that maintainers may decline a contribution, and that this is not a
  judgement of the person who wrote it

Things that are not acceptable:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks
- harassment, public or private
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) if you would rather
not do it in public, or by opening an issue if you would.

Reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and in confidence. Maintainers
may remove comments, commits, code, and issues that do not follow this policy,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
